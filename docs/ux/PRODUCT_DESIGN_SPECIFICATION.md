# Product design specification

The cockpit is a research laboratory, not a trading terminal. Follow a single question: does a model add measurable predictive value compared with simple statistical references under the same protocol?

| Screen | Primary task | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Overview | Establish experiment status and main finding | Matched report, campaign progress, next steps |
| Data | Understand provenance and causal inputs | Aligned price/feature, chronological partitions, correlations |
| Training | Define and execute a reproducible experiment | Five-step protocol, budget estimate, progress, loss and checkpoint |
| Evaluation | Compare like-for-like evidence | Model/baseline loss, paired intervals, seeds, regimes and costs |
| Replay | Understand an individual decision | Revealed candles, recorded fills, portfolio, deterministic policy |
| Explainability | Understand measured input sensitivity | Perturbation results and limitations |

Presentation stages reuse selected artifacts; do not train or re-evaluate automatically. An explicitly requested replay generation remains available. A missing report is missing evidence, never a synthetic number.
